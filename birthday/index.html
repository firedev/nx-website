My birthday

29–30 August 2026 · Phuket

Hi, friend. I invite you to my birthday, stretched over the weekend. Saturday we take a boat out of Chalong, freedive until the golden hour, then land at The Nakara Hill Villa and party all night. Sunday: breakfast and sauna at Kaif.

Tell me you're in Or call

Saturday on the water

The day follows the sun: blue water till evening, gold light at the villa.

  1. The night before Sleep well

    Saturday runs from ten in the morning till sunrise. Come rested.

  2. 10:00 Meet at Chalong pier

    We load up at Chalong pier and head out. The boat leaves at ten sharp.

  3. Day Psychedelic dives

    Slow and deep, off the buoy. Everyone on this boat freedives, an instructor and a national champion included.

  4. 14:00 Nikita takes the villa

    Check-in opens at two. Nikita (Mønyc) lands first and rigs the sound while we're still on the water.

  5. Sunset We come ashore

    We wrap up with the light and the party moves to The Nakara Hill Villa.

  6. Night Ecstatic dance all night

    Crash at the villa (it's ours till Monday), or meet us at Kaif the next day. Dry clothes required either way.

Sunday the slow one

Recovery is part of the program.

  1. Around noon Meet at Kaif

    Whenever you surface, roll to Kaif Phuket. No one hurries anywhere.

  2. Then Breakfast and sauna

    Solid food first, steam after. Saturday leaves through the skin.

  3. 18:00 Exclusive ecstatic dance

    A set by Mønyc Music, streamed live from the villa. The weekend ends where it peaked.

Monday we surface

  1. 12:00 Checkout

    Sleep the weekend off, then hand back the keys at noon. Collect your USB sticks, your dry clothes and your stories. The villa keeps nothing.

Get your USB sticks

The decks are up at the villa all night. Bring your set and play it.

Bring a change of clothes

You'll step off the boat wet and salty, and the party runs till sunrise. Pack dry clothes for the night, whether you crash at the villa or head to Kaif in the morning.
